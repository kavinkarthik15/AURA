# Policy Benchmark V1

## Policy Version

- Policy Version: `policy_v1`
- Training Date: recorded in the policy artifact and policy registry
- Model Type: empirical experience-frequency policy

## Dataset

- Dataset Source: execution experiences in `backend/data/experience_dataset.json`
- Dataset Size: 171 experience records at the v0.10 baseline
- Policy Samples: one sample per recorded action

## Metrics

- Top-1 Accuracy: generated by `PolicyEvaluator`
- Average Probability: generated by `PolicyEvaluator`
- Coverage: proportion of samples for which the policy has supporting action evidence

The benchmark result is stored with each policy entry in `backend/ai/policy_registry.json`, together with the dataset hash, sample count, and training timestamp.

## Interpretation

The policy is intentionally empirical in Sprint 11.1. It learns action support from successful and unsuccessful execution records and supplies a probability-like ranking signal to beam search. These metrics establish a reproducible baseline for later strategy discovery and uncertainty-aware planning.

## Future Comparison

Sprint 12 may add richer confidence calibration and uncertainty analysis. Neural, transformer, or graph-based policy models are deferred to Sprint 13 or later.
